# Reconciliation: correcting the "it's Shield's law" ruling

**Date:** 2026-09-07
**Author:** Stacey (via reconciliation session)

## What happened

Three reconciliation records are duplicated between this repo and
[shield-canon](https://github.com/Solid-Stride/shield-canon) (kept in
both because corpus docs on each side cite them): `2026-09-07-core-not-pearl.md`,
`2026-09-07-four-pads-vs-rejected-numerology.md`, and
`2026-09-07-no-fixed-four-option-model.md`.

When the two copies were found to have diverged (a cross-reference
sweep replaced precise links with prose in this repo's copies, while
shield-canon's kept precise-but-broken relative links), the product
owner was asked which copy should be treated as authoritative. The
initial answer — "it's Shield's law" — was recorded as shield-canon's
copies winning, with this repo's copies marked secondary.

**That was wrong.** Corrected: **this repo's (Luma's) copies are
authoritative.** All three records are fundamentally about
LumaShield's own interaction grammar and product-level choices (Core
vs. Pearl naming for Luma's Shield anatomy, and LumaShield's own 4-Pad
layout), not about the broader Shield architecture — the same
category error the whole Luma/Shield split exists to prevent, just
occurring in the reconciliation records' own metadata rather than in
product content.

## Additional correction: "undecided," not "deliberately open"

While correcting this, a related framing error surfaced in
`2026-09-07-no-fixed-four-option-model.md` and
[shield-anatomy.md](../corpus/architecture/shield-anatomy.md): both
described universal Shield's Pad count as "left open by design" —
implying flexibility was itself a settled architectural principle.

**Corrected, per direct confirmation:** LumaShield is locked at 4
Pads — settled, not a placeholder. Universal Shield's Pad count is
**undecided** — no ruling has been made either way. "Undecided" and
"deliberately flexible by design" are different claims; only the
former is currently true. Decision 5 of the Volume II Approval
Addendum (*"no universal attention score or fixed four-option model
governs every Shield"*) rules out treating four as a proven universal
law — it does not establish that Shield has affirmatively chosen
flexibility as a design principle.

## Resolution

- All three duplicated reconciliation records: **this repo's copy is
  authoritative.** Notices corrected in each file.
- `shield-anatomy.md`: reworded from "universal SHIELD leaves the
  option/Pad count open per-Shield" to "undecided," and from
  "LumaShield specifically has chosen four" to "LumaShield is locked
  at 4 Pads — this is settled."

## Outstanding

shield-canon's own copies of these three records still carry the

docs may still use the "left open by design" framing for the Pad
count. This needs to be relayed to that repo so it can correct its
copies symmetrically — not something this repo can fix unilaterally.
