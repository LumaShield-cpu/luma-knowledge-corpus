# Research & validation framework (context, not architecture)

**Source:** [SHIELD Architecture Series, Volume V: Research & Validation Framework](../../sources/docs/shield-architecture-series-volume-v-research-and-validation-framework.pdf.meta.md).
Self-marked "Current Working Framework v0.2 — Research Draft."

**Status: no new architecture.** This volume's own closing statement:
*"No new architecture is introduced in Volume V. No existing
architecture is renamed or reinterpreted."* It's an academic research
methodology document (styled after NSERC/Mitacs grant proposals), not
a source of product facts — captured briefly here for context, not at
the depth of Volumes II/III.

## Why this is still worth recording

SHIELD's own architecture series treats its central claims as
**unproven hypotheses, not settled fact**:

> Human coordination in complex operational environments may be
> improved by translating operational knowledge into adaptive,
> context-aware interaction models that reduce the cognitive effort
> required to understand, decide, coordinate, and act.

Explicitly framed as falsifiable — "studies may find that translation
is partial, that adaptation is insufficient, that cognitive load
reduction is marginal" — and all such outcomes are described as
scientifically valid, not failures. This is a useful epistemic-humility
marker for the whole corpus: architecture docs describe intent and
design; this volume is a reminder that whether the design actually
achieves its stated benefits is, by the project's own admission, still
an open empirical question.

## The six subsidiary research questions

Reduce navigation burden? Improve task completion via adaptive
composition? Does a stable interaction grammar hold up cross-domain?
Does operational memory improve decision continuity across personnel
changes? Does temporary shared context improve trust between
unfamiliar parties? Does AI-mediated intent resolution reduce
communication overhead? — none answered here, all posed as things to
test.

## Planned validation sites (not yet run, per this document)

- **BJD Electric** — a small industrial electrical contractor; planned
  4+ week pre/post-deployment baseline study on field-office
  coordination, safety-process adherence, decision memory, and
  administrative burden.
- **Professional Shield, multi-domain** — legal, real estate,
  accounting, and safety consulting practitioners, testing
  cross-domain applicability of the Professional Shield model already
  described in [composition-engine.md](composition-engine.md).
- **HomeShield** — consumer-side validation (furnace failure response,
  pool maintenance, tree/arborist coordination) testing generalization
  beyond professional/industrial settings.

## Consequence for this corpus

Treat every performance or outcome claim about SHIELD (reduced
cognitive load, improved coordination, etc.) as **design intent**, not
a demonstrated result, unless a specific study result gets ingested
later. The architecture describes what the system is built to do; this
volume is the project's own acknowledgment that it hasn't yet
demonstrated that it does it.
