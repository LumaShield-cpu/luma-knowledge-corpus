# Glossary

Terms whose meaning has drifted, been overloaded, or been used
inconsistently across docs/tickets/chats. Each entry pins one current
meaning. Add a term here as soon as you notice two sources disagree on what
it means — that disagreement is itself a form of drift worth recording.

Format:

```
### Term
**Means:** the current, agreed definition.
**Not to be confused with:** other senses this word has been used in, and
where (link to source/reconciliation record).
```
