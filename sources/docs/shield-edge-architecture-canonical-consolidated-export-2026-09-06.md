## Canonical Declaration

**SHIELD is computational human coordination infrastructure. SHIELD Edge is a context-governed coordination runtime that resolves operational meaning, actors, authority, dependencies, capabilities, evidence, and human resolution into adaptive multi-party operational experiences at the edge.**

  

SHIELD Edge is not primarily interface infrastructure with coordination added. It is coordination infrastructure whose human interface is dynamically rendered from governed context.

  

Its stable operating chain is:

  

Authorized / authoritative source systems

        ↓

Translation Engine Volume I

        ↓

Released governed operational meaning / OCPs

        ↓

SHIELD Edge verification, transport and eligibility

        ↓

Context Resolution + IADR + SCK

        ↓

Operational Program + capabilities + coordination

        ↓

AMC semantic composition

        ↓

URK / device realization

        ↓

Sovereign human judgment / HRD where required

        ↓

Governed events, evidence and source-system mutation

        ↓

Candidate-first return to Volume I when new knowledge is proposed

### Human First Constitutional Principle

Humans remain responsible for judgment, expertise, accountability and resolution. SHIELD owns the burden of locating, assembling, routing, synchronizing and presenting the context required for humans to coordinate effectively.

### Foundational Runtime Principle

**The operational model is primary. The rendered interface is a transient projection of governed runtime state. Rendering is never the source of operational truth.**

  

# 1\. Constitutional Boundaries

## 1.1 Translation Engine Volume I versus SHIELD Edge Volume II

  - **Volume I** establishes governed operational meaning, Released Graph Assertions, authoritative OKG state, OCP creation, release, supersession and revocation.
  - **SHIELD Edge** governs runtime verification, freshness, eligibility, context resolution, Effective Authority, capability binding, orchestration, rendering, coordination, Human Gates, HRDs, provisional mutation and runtime suspension/abstention.
  - Runtime evidence, HRDs, observations, exceptions and field intelligence return to Volume I as **candidate evidence**, never as self-promoted knowledge.
  - Edge provenance extends Volume I provenance; it never becomes a competing authoritative Operational Knowledge Graph.
  - Cache admission, local preload, eviction, offline persistence and runtime suspension do not change Volume I knowledge status.
  - Volume I RELEASED/EFFECTIVE does not imply Edge ELIGIBLE.
  - Edge ABSTAINED/SUSPENDED does not imply Volume I REJECTED/REVOKED.

## 1.2 Canonical §90 Mapping Fidelity

Every connector/mapping is purpose-specifically classified as one of:

  

LOSSLESS

LOSSLESS\_WITH\_METADATA

LOSSY\_BUT\_GOVERNED

READ\_ONLY\_SEMANTIC

UNSUPPORTED

  

The classification applies across ingestion, OCP delivery, authorized knowledge views, Context Projections, event-return mappings and system-of-record write-back. Successful serialization is never sufficient evidence of semantic equivalence.

## 1.3 Authority and Meaning Separation

knowledge authority

≠ runtime eligibility

≠ identity assurance

≠ operational authority

≠ administrative authority

≠ commercial entitlement

≠ certification

≠ physical reality

  

No layer may substitute for another.

## 1.4 Core Governance Outcomes

The SHIELD Constitutional Kernel resolves consequential runtime governance into:

  

PERMIT

PERMIT\_WITH\_CONDITIONS

DEFER

ABSTAIN

DENY

INVALIDATE

  

ABSTAIN means SHIELD cannot safely establish eligibility. DENY means the action is known to be prohibited.

## 1.5 Consequence Classes

C0 — informational

C1 — derived / advisory

C2 — reversible operational mutation

C3 — consequential operational mutation

C4 — human resolution / regulated action

  

Higher consequence requires stronger governance, evidence, authority and resolution semantics.

## 1.6 No Constitutional Superuser

No platform administrator, tenant administrator, support operator, model, package, connector, commercial account or emergency mode is constitutionally entitled to bypass tenant isolation, policy, provenance, Human Resolution or authority.

  

# 2\. Accepted Foundational Runtime Model

## 2.1 Window

A **Window** is the complete active SHIELD workspace on one physical or virtual display surface.

  

Canonical geometries:

  

1×1

2×1

1×2

2×2

  

One Frame is the default. Every additional visible Frame must earn its presence through operational relevance.

## 2.2 Frame

A **Frame** is an independently governed operational execution surface. It carries its own CEE, semantic role, Module runtime, capability bindings, processor graph, evidence state, interaction state, rendering state, coordination state, provenance and lifecycle.

  

Frame semantic roles:

  

CORE

SUPPORT

EVIDENCE

ACTION

REFERENCE

COORDINATION

AMBIENT

  

Frame retention classes:

  

PINNED

PERSISTENT

CONTEXTUAL

TRANSIENT

EPHEMERAL

  

Human pinning can preserve preference, never stale authority or invalid context.

## 2.3 Semantic Priority, Human Focus and Prominence

These remain distinct:

  

  - **Frame Semantic Priority** — operational significance.
  - **Human Focus** — where immediate attention is directed.
  - **Frame Prominence** — visual/interaction weight assigned by AMC.

  

Focus never rewrites priority; visual prominence never becomes operational authority.

## 2.4 Composition Stability

AMC preserves spatial continuity and resists unnecessary adaptation. Recomposition is a governed **Window Composition Transaction (WCT)**:

  

PROPOSED → EVALUATING → VALIDATED → STAGED → COMMITTED

  

with REJECTED, DEFERRED, ABSTAINED and ROLLED\_BACK alternatives.

## 2.5 Human Cognitive and Interruption Budgets

  - **HCB — Human Cognitive Budget** constrains visible complexity.
  - **HIB — Human Interruption Budget** constrains unnecessary interruption.

  

Safety-critical governed attention may supersede ordinary HIB limits, but noncritical interface activity must not create alert or composition storms.

## 2.6 Module

A **Module** is a declarative semantic interaction unit rendered inside a Frame. One Primary Module defines Frame purpose; Auxiliary Modules may enrich it but cannot independently redefine purpose, authority, lifecycle or Human Resolution.

  

Module families include:

  

Information

Capture

Spatial

Analytical

Procedural

Decision

Coordination

External Surface

  

Modules request semantic capabilities and never directly invoke vendor implementations.

## 2.7 Capability, Provider, Connector and Processor

  - **Capability** — governed semantic operation.
  - **Capability Provider** — implementation of a Capability Contract.
  - **Connector** — governed adapter between SHIELD semantics and an external authoritative system/service.
  - **Processor** — bounded computation over declared inputs producing typed outputs with provenance.

  

Canonical rule:

  

**Processors compute. Governed capabilities mutate.**

## 2.8 SHIELD Edge Package (SEP)

A **SEP** is a signed/versioned/policy-declared distribution unit containing capabilities, processors, optional Modules, schemas, runtime resources and metadata. Enterprise-private packages are first-class. Installation makes capability discoverable; contextual binding makes it active.

## 2.9 Compute Rail and Dock

  - **Compute Rail** is a contextual computational capability surface, not an application launcher.
  - **Dock** contains Operational Programs, not conventional applications.

## 2.10 Context Execution Envelope (CEE)

The CEE is the bounded runtime contract around a Frame and operational context. It resolves intent, OCPs, actor, Effective Authority, state, dependencies, capabilities, processor bindings, rendering constraints, coordination, provenance and Human Resolution requirements.

  

A CEE is assembled contextually; it is not copied as transferable authority between humans/devices.

## 2.11 Event Fabric

The **Event Fabric** is a governed operational event fabric, not an ungoverned message bus. Events are immutable, typed and provenance-bound. Causality and correlation are explicit. Frames and Modules communicate through typed events, never arbitrary direct commands or shared mutable state.

## 2.12 SHIELD Connect

SHIELD Connect is a native context-bound coordination protocol. It transports structured coordination requests, evidence, advice, review, handoff, acknowledgement, oversight and resolution context. The recipient receives a recipient-specific CEE; sender authority is never copied.

## 2.13 Edge SLM and Large Reasoning Providers

The **Edge SLM** is a local reasoning governor, not the deterministic controller or policy engine. Heavy reasoning may be delegated provider-neutrally to Copilot, Gemini, OpenAI, private or future models through bounded **Reasoning Delegation Envelopes (RDEs)**.

  

External reasoning:

  

  - receives minimum necessary context;
  - cannot subscribe directly to unrestricted Event Fabric state;
  - returns interpreted/proposed output;
  - cannot mutate runtime truth;
  - cannot create authority or HRD;
  - cannot become the final policy engine.

## 2.14 Human Resolution Decision (HRD)

Canonical HRD definition:

  

**A tamper-evident, provenance-bound record of an authorized human's consequential resolution, including exact context, evidence, authority, options, decision, time, environment, resulting state, and correction/escalation links.**

  

“Cryptographically bound HRD” remains noncanonical until an independently approved cryptographic assurance specification supports that exact claim.

# §§1–210 — Data, Knowledge & Context Transport Protocol v0.1

### Purpose

Defines how SHIELD transports operational meaning, state, evidence and control without collapsing their semantic distinctions or transferring more context than a consumer requires.

### Four information planes

KNOWLEDGE — OCPs, procedures, rules, semantic models

TRANSACTION — live operational state, events and mutations

EVIDENCE — source, derived, interpreted and attested artifacts

CONTROL — CEE, identity projections, Governance Decisions, MAEs, HRDs

### Canonical objects

  - **SOO — Semantic Operational Object:** source-neutral operational entity.
  - **OSO — Operational State Object:** normalized live state with source authority and freshness.
  - **DAM — Data Authority Map:** property-level source authority.
  - **Context Transport Envelope (CTE):** governed movement of bounded semantic context.
  - **EIP — Edge Identity Projection:** minimum authority-relevant identity projection.
  - **CPJ — Context Projection:** purpose-bound consumer-specific subset of a CEE.
  - **CCT — Context Continuation Token:** cross-device/session continuity without transferable authority.
  - **OA — Operational Artifact:** governed content object with provenance and classification.
  - **ECC / EKC:** local cached context/knowledge representations.
  - **PMR — Pending Mutation Record:** provisional operational-effect request, never premature truth.
  - **CRX:** reconciliation/conflict handling for disconnected or concurrent state.
  - **GKE / Transport Resolver:** governed knowledge/context movement.
  - **Transport Receipt / Context Transport Ledger:** evidence of movement, not proof of semantic truth.

### Controlling invariants

  - Transport never promotes epistemic status.
  - Source authority can be property-specific; no universal SoR is assumed.
  - Context minimization is mandatory.
  - Sidecars may extend meaning but cannot create shadow systems of record.
  - Offline operation cannot widen authority, fabricate freshness or suppress conflict.
  - Cached authoritative content retains exact version, provenance, release status and expiry.
  - CEE continuity is reconstructed and revalidated on the receiving device; authority is never copied.
  - PMRs remain provisional until source confirmation; queued HRDs are not perpetual permission.
  - Canonical §90 mapping fidelity governs both read and write paths.

  

# §§211–243 — Cross-Volume Constitutional Alignment Amendment

### Purpose

Formally separates Translation Engine Volume I knowledge authority from SHIELD Edge runtime authority without reopening §§1–210.

### Core rulings

  - Volume I owns Released Graph Assertions, authoritative OKG state, OCP release/supersession/revocation and knowledge promotion/rejection.
  - Edge owns verification, runtime eligibility, context resolution, capability binding, rendering, orchestration, coordination, Human Gates and runtime suspension/abstention.
  - Runtime feedback returns candidate-first.
  - HRD authority is bounded to the human resolution it records; it does not automatically become general future knowledge.
  - Edge may locally suspend a still-released artifact; only Volume I changes authoritative knowledge lifecycle.
  - Validated/Approved/Effective/Rejected/Revoked belong primarily to Volume I knowledge lifecycle. Edge uses Verified/Fresh/Eligible/Suspended/Abstained/Expired for runtime state.
  - Reconnect sequence prioritizes revocation/supersession and current authority before resuming consequential work.

### Amendment declaration

Sections 211–243 clarify and complete the cross-volume boundary. They do not renumber or diminish §§1–210. The completed canonical §90 remains sole §90.

  

# §§244–469 — Adaptive Matrix Controller & Human Experience Composition Protocol v0.1

### Purpose

Defines the protected composition intelligence that turns governed runtime state into a bounded human experience.

### Canonical model

  - **AMC** is protected composition intelligence; the SLM is advisory and never commits composition independently.
  - **SEM — Semantic Experience Manifest** describes device-independent semantic experience requirements.
  - One Window contains at most four visible Frames and defaults to 1×1.
  - Every additional Frame requires operational justification.
  - Frame roles, retention, semantic priority, human focus and prominence remain separate.
  - Composition is transactionally committed through WCT.
  - Human Cognitive Budget and Interruption Budget constrain complexity and interruption.
  - Spatial continuity and composition stability are preferred unless operational consequence requires change.
  - A Frame that becomes invalid can remain visible in explanatory/read-only form while prohibited actions are removed.
  - AMC cannot fabricate authority, business meaning, evidence quality or brand identity.

### Core principle

**The user should not navigate the software; governed operational context should compose the experience around the human objective.**

  

# §§470–581 — Universal Rendering Kernel & Device Execution Protocol v0.1

### Purpose

Defines device-independent semantic experience realization across phones, foldables, tablets, desktops, industrial terminals, wall displays, wearables and AR/spatial devices.

### Canonical objects

  - **URK — Universal Rendering Kernel** maps SEM into a device-specific realization.
  - **DEA — Device Execution Adapter** exposes the capabilities and constraints of a device/environment.
  - Rendering capability negotiation considers display, input, sensors, accessibility, environmental state and resource limits.

### Interaction grammar

The protected SHIELD grammar comprises **Core, Ring, Pads, Dial and Adaptive Operational Briefing (AOB)**. AOB is composite rather than a fifth peer primitive.

### Invariants

  - Semantic invariance, rendering adaptation.
  - Device adaptation may not remove information required for valid judgment.
  - A device that cannot faithfully render a required decision context must Abstain or redirect rather than degrade past validity.
  - UI pixels are never runtime truth.
  - Accessibility can override visual brand purity but never operational meaning.

  

# §§582–767 — SLM, Reasoning Federation & Agentic Governance Protocol v0.1

### Purpose

Defines AI reasoning as bounded advisory computation under deterministic SHIELD governance.

### Canonical components

  - **Edge SLM Reasoning Service** — local reasoning governor.
  - **WCE — Working Context Envelope** — bounded model working context.
  - **Instruction Trust Model** — separates trusted runtime instruction from untrusted content.
  - **Capability Mediation Gateway / GXP** — models request bounded capabilities rather than hold unrestricted tools.
  - **Reasoning Provider Federation** — provider-neutral routing among local, enterprise and external models.
  - **RDE — Reasoning Delegation Envelope** — minimum-purpose packet for external reasoning.

### Invariants

  - No LLM is the policy engine.
  - Models never directly mutate authoritative state.
  - Agentic state is externalized; model context is disposable.
  - Models do not receive unrestricted credentials or tool access.
  - Prompt injection is treated as a cross-boundary security threat.
  - Model admission is task/profile-specific.
  - Outputs retain method class and epistemic status (LLM\_INFERENCE, ML\_INFERENCE, etc.).
  - Agreement among models does not establish truth.
  - AI cannot create HRD, external approval, certification or authority.

# §§913–1054 — Human Resolution, Decision Evidence & Consequential Action Protocol v0.1

### Purpose

Defines deliberate accountable human resolution for consequential decisions.

### HRD

An HRD binds the authorized human, exact decision context, evidence, authority, options, decision, time, environment, resulting state and correction/escalation relationships.

### Human Gates

Human Gates may be conditional, required or multi-party. They cannot be bypassed by AI or replaced by vague consent interactions. Multi-person resolution preserves each accountable human decision independently.

### Invariants

  - Human Resolution is not a button click.
  - Advice, acknowledgement, approval, responsibility acceptance and resolution are distinct.
  - A decision may expire or be invalidated when its governing context materially changes.
  - Consequential mutation occurs only after required Human Resolution and MAE/governance checks.
  - HRDs do not self-promote into general operational knowledge.

# §§1202–1412 — Security, Cryptographic Assurance, Trust Zones & Runtime Integrity Protocol v0.1

  - Offline/air-gapped operation must preserve the same semantic security pipeline.
  - Sensitive context is minimized before egress.
  - No package or model can weaken T0 constitutional controls.

# §§1598–1760 — Operational Program, Workflow, State Machine & Program Composition Protocol v0.1

### Purpose

Defines the executable semantic runtime structure that replaces conventional application navigation.

### Operational Program

An Operational Program is not an app, OCP, workflow or SoR. It binds released operational meaning to runtime states, workflows, capabilities, Human Gates, composition patterns, dependencies and completion semantics.

### Invariants

  - OPDs have explicit entry/exit, state machine, workflow, exception, recovery, capability and Human Gate definitions.
  - One Program may produce many Window compositions over time.
  - Foreground/background/ambient Program state is separate from OS process state.
  - Program completion is defined semantically, not by closing UI.
  - Cross-program capabilities can execute without forcing cross-program navigation.
  - Program versions and OCP versions remain explicitly bound.

  - Lowest price cannot overcome mandatory technical/safety/legal/quality requirements.
  - Supplier proposed equivalent is candidate, not approved substitute.
  - Procurement authority is scoped; budget existence, title or system access does not create spend authority.
  - PO authorization ≠ issuance ≠ supplier acknowledgement.
  - ASN ≠ proof of shipment; carrier delivery ≠ buyer receipt; receipt ≠ acceptance; acceptance ≠ operational availability.
  - Ordered, shipped, received, accepted, returned and remaining quantities remain separate.
  - Three-way match ≠ quality acceptance ≠ payment authority.
  - Emergency procurement uses preauthorized alternate controls; later paperwork cannot fabricate retroactive authority.
  - A second supplier is continuity-ready only when qualification, compatibility, commercial path, credentials and logistics are actually established.
  - Recall/traceability applies to actual affected lots/serials/configurations and preserves historical use.

  

  

# 4\. Cross-Protocol Architectural Invariants

The following invariants control the architecture as a whole and should be treated as implementation guardrails even where a specific protocol is not named explicitly.

  

1.  **Operational meaning precedes presentation.**
2.  **Rendering is a consequence of runtime state, never a source of runtime state.**
3.  **Humans retain judgment and accountable resolution for consequential decisions.**
4.  **No AI model is the final policy engine or authority source.**
5.  **Knowledge release, runtime eligibility, authority, entitlement, certification and physical reality are distinct domains.**
6.  **Authority is contextual, action-specific, revocable and continuously reevaluated.**
7.  **Offline operation never increases authority or epistemic certainty.**
8.  **Context is minimized to the purpose and consumer.**
9.  **Derived artifacts never overwrite source artifacts.**
10. **Probabilistic interpretation never silently becomes deterministic fact.**
11. **Interaction state remains provisional until governed authoritative commit.**
12. **Source-system mutation requires scoped authorization, idempotency/reconciliation and actual confirmation.**
13. **Timeout, cancellation, delivery, acceptance and completion are distinct states.**
14. **Conflict is preserved and resolved explicitly rather than normalized by last-write-wins.**
15. **Caches, replicas, projections and sidecars never acquire authority simply by persistence.**
16. **No package, administrator, customer tier, emergency state or model may bypass constitutional controls.**
17. **Simulation, prediction, analytics and memory remain distinguishable from observed current state.**
18. **Historical reconstruction uses the rules, versions and context actually present at the historical time.**
19. **Service restoration is distinct from operational consequence resolution.**
20. **Business continuity preserves accountable coordination without becoming governance suspension.**
21. **Commercial rights can only narrow runtime eligibility; they never create authority.**
22. **External legal/regulatory authority remains external to SHIELD.**
23. **Organizational role/title/position are authority inputs, never authority by themselves.**
24. **Physical representation never substitutes for physical reality.**
25. **Supply-chain transactional states remain separate from physical receipt, acceptance and operational eligibility.**

# 7\. Final Canonical Summary

SHIELD Edge is a universal operational composition and coordination runtime for headless and sufficiently accessible software. Its architecture decouples operational meaning from source-system UI without claiming ownership of source-system business logic or authoritative data. Translation Engine Volume I releases governed operational meaning. SHIELD Edge verifies and transports that meaning, intersects it with live state, identity, Effective Authority, dependencies, policy, evidence, device/environment conditions and commercial/external constraints, and creates bounded Context Execution Envelopes. Operational Programs use those envelopes to bind semantic capabilities, processors, connectors, human participants and Human Gates. The Adaptive Matrix Controller composes the minimum necessary multi-Frame human experience, while the Universal Rendering Kernel realizes the same semantic experience across devices. Models may reason, summarize, classify and propose, but deterministic governance remains outside the model and humans retain consequential judgment. Every meaningful transformation, assertion, decision and mutation remains provenance-bound; conflicts and uncertainty survive transport; offline operation never enlarges authority; and runtime events return new observations to Volume I only as candidates. The architecture extends through identity, security, storage, configuration, attention, governed release, administration, assurance, service operation, continuity, commercial governance, legal/external obligations, workforce, physical resources and procurement without allowing any of those layers to substitute for another's authority. SHIELD therefore coordinates the human, semantic, computational, organizational and physical dimensions of work while preserving the exact distinction among what is known, what is observed, what is permitted, what is available, what has actually happened and what a human is accountable to decide.
