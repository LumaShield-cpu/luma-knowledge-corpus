The following propositions are **frozen** as controlling working-architecture commitments for prototype validation and Volume II reconstruction:

1. A Shield is an OCP-driven governed human coordination environment, not a fixed interface grammar.
2. Every datum and operational meaning rendered by SHIELD must be contained in, derived from, or received through a contract governed by the active OCP.  
   *(Approved with person-input clarification recorded in §6.1 — the freeze stands; the clarification governs interpretation.)*
3. Shield Packets are essential bounded runtime carriers and are the meaningful unit behind taps, voice, sensors, connector events, transitions, and handoffs.
4. SHIELD Logic establishes semantic relevance, eligibility, precedence, alternatives, consequence, and closure before a renderer establishes spatial or conversational form.
5. Option order is a contextual, constraint-first partial order; no universal attention score or fixed four-option model governs every Shield.
6. Packs, Stacks, and Paths are optional structures in universal SHIELD, while Packs and Stacks are essential candidate structures for future LumaShield.
8. The rendering constitution defines behavioural laws; frameworks, templates, components, and renderers remain replaceable.
9. The **STACEY OS** OCP-to-prototype capability should become the reference implementation and regression corpus for Volume II.  
   *(Nomenclature corrected from “STCOS” per approval note — see §6.3.)*
10. The computability paper remains a research-origin manuscript and cannot govern architecture until its claims are formalized, falsifiable, and validated.

## 6. Architectural corrections incorporated by approval

### 6.1 Person-supplied data and Decision 2 (clarification)

**Decision 2 remains approved and frozen**, with the following controlling clarification:

Person interaction with a Shield does not create an exception that lets ungovered raw input become operational meaning outside the OCP. The person may **supply** information; the OCP governs what may be accepted and how it may be used.

**Controlling statement:**

> **Person-supplied data:** The OCP governs permitted input type, purpose, handling, authority, and consequences—not the literal information before the person provides it.

Implications for prototype work:

- Before input exists, SHIELD may present OCP-governed prompts, fields, modalities, and constraints.
- After the person provides information, that information becomes operational only under the active OCP’s contract for type, purpose, handling, authority, retention, visibility, and consequence.
- Rendered meaning, eligibility, options, and closure remain OCP-contracted (Decision 2), including meaning derived from person-supplied input.

### 6.2 Identity authority (Decision 7 quarantine + correction)

SHIELD **must not** impose one universal sovereign identity root as a controlling requirement for all Shields.

**Controlling statement (incorporated):**

> Identity authority comes from the governing context. Enterprise Shields use institutional identity systems such as Microsoft Entra. LumaShield may use a person-governed identity model. Packets carry only the minimum purpose-bound identity and authorization projection, while Stacks preserve boundaries among identity providers and contexts.

Implications for prototype work:

- Enterprise Shields may resolve identity and authorization against institutional providers (e.g., Microsoft Entra) as the governing context requires.
- LumaShield may explore a person-governed identity model without forcing that model onto enterprise Shields.
- Packets remain minimization surfaces: purpose-bound identity and authorization projection only.
- Stacks (where used) preserve boundaries among identity providers and contexts; they do not merge providers into a single unrestricted profile.
- Final constitutional naming of any personal identity root (e.g., “Luma ID”) remains an open question outside this baseline freeze.

### 6.3 Nomenclature: STCOS → STACEY OS

**Controlling correction:** Replace **STCOS** with **STACEY OS** everywhere this baseline is cited, extended, or implemented.

This correction applies to:

- Decision 9 as frozen in §3.1;
- references to the OCP-to-prototype capability, regression corpus, and golden prototype recovery work;
- future Volume II reconstruction prose derived from this baseline.

The frozen v0.2 source bytes are **not** rewritten; readers and downstream work products apply this nomenclature correction by reference to this addendum.
